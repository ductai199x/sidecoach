Piece sets shipped with Sidecoach

The pieces directly in this folder are Sidecoach's own (2026-09-30), made for Sidecoach and
distributed with it under the same terms as the rest of Sidecoach. How they were made:
tools/pieces/README.md (concept sheets generated with OpenAI's gpt-image-2.5 from our own
brief, traced to SVG). They follow the Staunton pattern of 1849, in the public domain.

Each folder below holds an unmodified copy of a set from the Lichess repository
(https://github.com/lichess-org/lila, public/piece/<set>/), under the licence its author gave
it, as Lichess's COPYING.md lists it. These licences cover those piece files only.
