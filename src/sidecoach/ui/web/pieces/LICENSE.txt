Sidecoach piece set
Sidecoach's own chess pieces (2026-09-30), made for Sidecoach and distributed with it under
the same terms as the rest of Sidecoach. No third-party artwork is included.

How they were made (tools/pieces/README.md): concept sheets generated with OpenAI's
gpt-image-2.5 from our own written brief, the chosen sheet (tools/pieces/round5-sheet.png)
segmented and traced to these SVGs by tools/pieces/to_svg.py. The pieces follow the Staunton
pattern of 1849, which is in the public domain.

The board colours are a plain green/cream palette and carry no third-party assets.
